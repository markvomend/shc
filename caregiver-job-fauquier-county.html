Job Summary

Join our dedicated team as an In Home Caregiver and make a meaningful difference in the lives of individuals requiring personalized care and support. In this vital role, you will provide compassionate assistance with daily activities, monitor health and safety, and foster a comfortable, respectful environment for clients across various care settings. Your energy, empathy, and attention to detail will help promote independence and enhance quality of life for those you serve. This paid position offers a rewarding opportunity to develop your caregiving skills while making a positive impact in your community.

Duties

  • Assist clients with activities of daily living (ADLs), including bathing, dressing, grooming, and mobility support using appropriate patient transfer methods such as Hoyer lifts or manual handling techniques.
  • Monitor patients’ vital signs, observe for changes in health status, and report findings accurately to healthcare professionals.
  • Prepare nutritious meals tailored to individual dietary needs and assist with feeding when necessary.
  • Administer medications following care plans and ensure compliance with HIPAA regulations to protect patient privacy.
  • Support clients with memory care needs, including dementia and Alzheimer’s care, ensuring safety and engagement through personalized activities.
  • Provide transportation assistance for medical appointments, errands, or community outings to promote social interaction and independence.
  • Maintain detailed documentation of patient care activities, update care plans regularly, and adhere to all safety protocols including first aid and CPR procedures.

Skills

  • Proven experience working with individuals with disabilities, developmental or intellectual disabilities support, or senior care in settings such as group homes, assisted living facilities, nursing homes, or home health environments.
  • Strong patient observation skills to detect changes in condition or behavior promptly.
  • Knowledge of medication administration, vital signs monitoring, and basic first aid/CPR certification.
  • Ability to perform heavy lifting safely using proper manual handling techniques; experience with Hoyer lifts is a plus.
  • Excellent communication skills for effective client interaction and collaboration with healthcare teams.
  • Familiarity with HIPAA regulations to ensure confidentiality and privacy of patient information.
  • Experience working with individuals with neurodevelopmental disorders or autism spectrum disorder is highly desirable.
  • Capable of assisting clients with personal transportation needs and supporting their participation in community activities.
  • Demonstrated ability to follow detailed care plans while maintaining flexibility to adapt to individual needs.

Join us as an In Home Caregiver where your dedication can truly transform lives! We value compassionate professionals committed to providing exceptional care that promotes dignity, independence, and well-being for every individual we serve.
