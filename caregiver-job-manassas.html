Overview

Join our compassionate team as a Personal Care Aide (PCA / CNA), where your dedication and skills will make a meaningful difference in the lives of individuals requiring support with daily activities and personal care. This vital role offers an opportunity to provide respectful, personalized assistance in various settings such as assisted living facilities, nursing homes, home health environments, and community-based programs. As a PCA / CNA, you will be a trusted caregiver, promoting independence, safety, and well-being through attentive and proactive care.

Duties

  • Assist residents or clients with activities of daily living (ADLs), including bathing, dressing, grooming, and toileting to ensure comfort and dignity.
  • Monitor patient health by taking vital signs such as blood pressure, pulse, temperature, and respiration; report any changes or concerns promptly.
  • Support mobility and transfers using proper techniques and equipment like Hoyer lifts or patient transfer methods to ensure safety for both the individual and caregiver.
  • Administer medications following care plans and HIPAA regulations to maintain health and safety standards.
  • Prepare nutritious meals tailored to dietary needs while maintaining cleanliness and hygiene standards in food handling.
  • Observe and document patient conditions meticulously, noting behavioral changes or symptoms related to dementia, Alzheimer's, autism, or other neurodevelopmental disorders.
  • Assist with medication administration, patient monitoring, documentation of care plans, and ensuring compliance with healthcare regulations such as HIPAA.
  • Support residents' emotional well-being through compassionate communication and engagement in activities that promote mental health.
  • Maintain a clean environment by performing light cleaning duties to uphold hygiene standards in living spaces.
  • Provide transportation assistance for appointments or community outings using appropriate safety procedures.

Skills

  • Proven experience working with individuals with developmental disabilities, intellectual disabilities, or neurodevelopmental disorders such as autism.
  • Strong knowledge of dementia care, memory care techniques, hospice care practices, and geriatrics.
  • Skilled in patient observation, monitoring vital signs, administering medications, and following detailed care plans.
  • Familiarity with assisted living environments, nursing homes, home health services, and long-term care settings.
  • Ability to operate patient transfer equipment like Hoyer lifts safely and effectively; manual handling expertise is essential.
  • Excellent communication skills for client interaction and team collaboration; understanding of HIPAA privacy standards is required.
  • Capable of heavy lifting and physical tasks involved in patient transfer methods while maintaining safety protocols.
  • Knowledge of first aid procedures including CPR certification to respond swiftly during emergencies.
  • Experience providing personal transportation assistance or supporting clients with mobility challenges outside the facility setting.
  • Competence in assisting with meal preparation while respecting dietary restrictions; supporting patients’ nutritional needs is key.

Join us to be part of a dedicated team committed to enriching lives through compassionate caregiving! Your skills will help foster independence while ensuring safety and comfort for those you serve.
