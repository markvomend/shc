Areas We Serve

Family-owned home care for seniors and their families across four Virginia counties.

Home Care Across Four Virginia Counties

The Shepherd Home Care is a family-owned agency based in Warrenton. We provide personal care, companion care, dementia care, respite care, and 24-hour care to seniors and their families in Fauquier, Prince William, Loudoun, and Fairfax counties.

Fauquier County

Our office is in Warrenton, the Fauquier County seat, at 70 Main Street, Suite 200. Communities we serve include Warrenton, Bealeton, Catlett, Calverton, Marshall, New Baltimore, Remington, and The Plains.

Prince William County

We provide in-home care throughout Prince William County, including Manassas, Manassas Park, Gainesville, Haymarket, Bristow, Nokesville, Woodbridge, Lake Ridge, Dale City, and Dumfries.

Loudoun County

We provide in-home care throughout Loudoun County, including Leesburg, Ashburn, Sterling, South Riding, Purcellville, Middleburg, Round Hill, and Lovettsville.

Fairfax County

We provide in-home care throughout Fairfax County, including Fairfax, Centreville, Chantilly, Clifton, Fairfax Station, Herndon, Reston, Vienna, Burke, and Springfield.

Not sure if we cover your area? Call us at 703-436-5000 and we'll let you know.
